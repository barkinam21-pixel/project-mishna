Older adult on frame left (approx. 55–65) and younger adult on frame right (approx. 25–35), both moving left-to-right. Quiet shared journey, thoughtful reserved expressions, credible adult anatomy, simple non-period-specific woven garments, practical sandals, small plain travel bundle and unmarked water vessel. Subtle warm pre-dawn rim light from behind-left, muted ochre earth and cool blue-violet dawn shadows, natural skin and cloth colors. Painterly cinematic semi-realism with hand-painted texture, slightly desaturated, restrained contrast, elegant visual depth and foreground/midground/background separation. Original 16:9 film composition, no exaggerated poses, no historical person or site identification, no scroll/tablet/book transfer, no architecture, no text or watermark. This is an explicitly **symbolic dramatization**, not a documented historical event.

**Negative constraints:** children, teenage faces, big cartoon eyes, cheerful children's-book illustration, glossy 3D plastic, hyperreal photograph, fantasy costumes, recognizable historical figures, Hebrew lettering, pseudo-text, invented temple, excessive dramatic rays, horror, extreme darkness, camera shake.
